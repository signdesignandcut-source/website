Easy from the first click

Small things that save time every day.

Always know what you've picked

The selected object tells you what it is: a star, a text, a group, a picture with its size and resolution.

Undo you can read

The undo and redo lists say what each step did, so you can go back to exactly the right place.

Change things on the page

Shapes keep their settings. Drag a handle to round a corner or add points to a star, and see it change as you go.

Type sizes the way you think

Number boxes understand units and sums: 5mm, 2ft 3in, 10*3 or +10%.

Text you can always edit

Type straight on the page with a text bar right beside your words. Text stays text, so you can double-click to change it later.

Just drag files in

Drop files, folders or zip files onto the window, or drag a file straight out of an e-mail attachment.

Your work is safe

Autosave brings your design back after a crash. Long jobs show their progress and can be cancelled.

See the cut before you cut

Preview the vinyl as the cutter sees it, drag the job into place, and play the cut in the simulator before you waste material.

Nothing to set up

One small installer, no extra downloads. Your activation code is filled in for you, and it keeps working without the internet.
